ASHEN CROWN — STAGE 2 SEPARATED ASSET PACK

Place this folder's contents under:
res://assets/environments/stage_2/

Folders:
background/      Far parallax artwork
midground/       Middle parallax artwork
foreground/      Large front scenery
tiles/           Tile/terrain atlas
platforms/       Platform atlas
obstacles/       Hazards
props/           Interactive/decorative props
architecture/    Broken structures
doors_gates/     Doors and gates
mechanical/      Chains/gears/mechanical details
details/         Small decals/debris
water/           Optional waterfall/liquid assets

IMPORTANT:
These are separated category PNGs cropped from the generated Stage 2
asset atlas. They are not all individually isolated sprites. Use the
tiles/platform sheets as atlases and the layered scenery as parallax
artwork. Do not import the original full overview as a gameplay texture.
