ASHEN CROWN — ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS

Target:
res://assets/effects/environment/
or res://assets/environments/hazards/

Each folder contains actual standalone PNG assets:
spikes, fire, toxic liquid, falling debris, steam vents, saw traps,
crushers, energy lasers, electric hazards, collapsing platforms,
explosive props, void rifts, thorns, moving pendulum hazards and
hazard particles.

These are gameplay hazard assets, not background art. Antigravity should
place their collision shapes separately from the visuals and use them
across the five stages where appropriate.

Keep pixel-art filtering on nearest and scale consistently with Cael.
